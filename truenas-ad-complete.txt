## Part 3: Active Directory Organizational Structure

### 3.1 Understanding Active Directory Delegation

**What is Delegation?**

Active Directory delegation is the process of assigning specific administrative permissions to users or groups without giving them full Domain Admin rights. This follows the **Principle of Least Privilege** - giving users only the permissions they need to do their job.

**Why Use Delegation?**

1. **Security**: Reduces risk by limiting who has full administrative access
2. **Compliance**: Meets regulatory requirements for separation of duties
3. **Efficiency**: Allows departmental admins to manage their own resources
4. **Accountability**: Creates clear audit trails of who can do what

**Common Delegation Scenarios:**

| Scenario | Permission Needed | Why |
|----------|-------------------|-----|
| Help desk reset passwords | Reset Password | Without giving ability to modify admin accounts |
| Server team join computers | Create/Delete Computer Objects | Without full domain control |
| Department manager | Manage specific OU | Without access to other departments |
| Application service account | Specific read/write | Without interactive login rights |

**In Our TrueNAS Setup:**

We create a service account (`truenas_join`) and delegate it the **specific permission to join computers to the domain**. This means:
- ✅ TrueNAS can join the domain automatically
- ✅ The account cannot change user passwords
- ✅ The account cannot modify group memberships
- ✅ The account cannot access email or files
- ❌ If compromised, damage is limited to computer object creation

This is **much more secure** than using the Administrator account for TrueNAS integration.

### 3.2 Create Organizational Units (OUs)

Organizational Units help organize and manage objects in Active Directory.

1. Open **Active Directory Users and Computers (ADUC)**
2. Right-click **truenas.local** → **New** → **Organizational Unit**
3. Create the following OUs:
   - **IT**
   - **Sales**
   - **Accounts**

**Result:** You should see three new OUs under your domain.
